ABOUT SHIPWRECKERS! (OVERBOARD! 1997)

Shipwreckers! (overboard! in europe, 1997) is psygnosis' naval action game: sail a pirate gunboat through top-down islands, broadside enemy ships, upgrade the hull and rescue the princess with a straight face. The two-player versus mode and the cartoon naval physics make it a genuine ps1 oddity worth keeping afloat.

No windows build exists, so the platform is ps1 - played through duckstation, rom sourced from romsfun.
