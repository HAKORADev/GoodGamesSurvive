ABOUT WEDDING DASH 2: RINGS AROUND THE WORLD

Wedding Dash 2: Rings Around the World, released on 23 june 2008, moved development from ImaginEngine to Sarbakan and the weddings from hometown halls to destination venues - each a new region with its own guest book. Quinn keeps planning, Flo keeps catering, and the seating chart gets meaner as the guest list grows stranger.

The sequel adds secondary objectives per venue and a wider cast of problem guests, but the core loop - seat, serve, soothe, chain - is the same five-minute engine the series runs on. It later reached steam on 12 august 2009 in playfirst's store batch, and that listing still sells the game.

That makes it the spin-off line's one official survivor: buyable on steam today, with the archive routes preserving the original-era builds next to it.
