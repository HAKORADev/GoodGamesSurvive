ABOUT CHICKEN INVADERS: REMASTERED

Chicken Invaders: Remastered is not a standalone game - it is InterAction studios' remaster line, and this page is its one honest address. The line is not a single product: it is a set of per-episode rebuilds of different ages and engines, and the shelf keeps them straight one by one.

The classic-engine remasters come first. Chicken Invaders 2: The Next Wave received its remaster on 26 January 2012 (the Christmas Edition's followed 20 December 2011) - HD graphics, orchestral music, an auto-fire function, progress saving and new languages. Chicken Invaders itself followed much later: its remaster beta opened 20 January 2023 with the full release on 31 January 2023, adding HD graphics, DirectX 11, mouse and controller support and the anniversary song from Universe. That rebuild ships only inside the studio's Steam package Chicken Invaders 1 (released there 7 March 2025), on the same shelf as the untouched 1999 original and the new Episode 1.

The Universe-engine reworks cover the whole main line. Every episode received a free beta remaster built on Chicken Invaders Universe content - redesigned graphics, new features - distributed through the studio's forum. When CIU update v149 turned that content into DLC, the beta servers shut down: the reworks still download, but now play as DLC inside Universe. The rework line continues as the Episode format, the studio's free major update announced in January 2025 for episodes 2-5 on a new engine. As of spring 2025 only Chicken Invaders Episode 1: The Saga Begins has an official Steam release; episodes 2-5 remain in beta on Steam and Google Play, and none have reached the App Store. A Chicken Invaders Collection for Xbox is dated 2026 on the series timeline.

The shelf law stays deliberate: the five classic pages remain the archive truth of the original builds - the exact installers, their sizes, their holiday editions. The remaster line is the modern way to play the same wars from a studio that is still alive and selling. Nothing here is a rescue; the upgrade path is one click away from every classic page that points here.
