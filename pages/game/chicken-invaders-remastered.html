ABOUT CHICKEN INVADERS: REMASTERED

Chicken Invaders: Remastered is the 2023 rebuild of the 1999 first war - and, unlike what older shelves used to claim, it is one specific game, not a line. Its beta opened on 20 January 2023 and the full release followed on 31 January 2023. The rebuild added a real main menu, HD graphics, a remastered cutscene, mouse and controller support, DirectX 11 for compatibility with windows 8 through 11, and the anniversary song from Chicken Invaders Universe. Like the original, it is a windows-only build.

Where it ships is part of the story. The remaster lives inside the studio's Steam package Chicken Invaders 1 (released there on 7 March 2025), which bundles three builds on one shelf: the untouched 1999 original, this 2023 remaster, and Episode 1: The Saga Begins - a brand-new version of the first war on the studio's new engine. The studio's own site distributes the same family through its download hub.

The shelf keeps the three builds straight: the 1999 classic page is the archive truth of the original installer era, this page is the classic-engine rebuild, and Episode 1 is the new-engine remake. Nothing here is a rescue - the studio is alive and selling - so the archive links ride along purely for preservation.
