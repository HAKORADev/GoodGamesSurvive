ABOUT CHICKEN INVADERS: REMASTERED

Chicken Invaders: Remastered is not a standalone game - it is InterAction studios' remaster line, and this page exists because the shelf needs one honest address for it. Every classic episode of the series received a rebuilt edition that carries all of its downloadable content with it: same wars, same waves, same six-weapon arsenal, redrawn for modern windows. The line is the direct upgrade of every old game on the Chicken Invaders shelf - which is exactly why those pages' UPGRADE lines point here instead of at each other's sequels.

The rollout stretched across a decade, one episode at a time. The Chicken Invaders 2 remaster shipped on 26 January 2012. The first game's remaster followed much later - dated 2023, it reached Steam inside the studio's 'Chicken Invaders 1' package (released there on 7 March 2025), sitting on the same store shelf as the untouched 1999 original and a beta remaster labeled Episode 1. Per Wikipedia, the whole main series is covered or being covered, with the newest episodes' remasters still riding the beta branch.

The shelf law stays deliberate: the five classic pages remain the archive truth of the original builds - the exact installers, their sizes, their holiday editions. The remaster line is the modern way to play the same wars, bought from a studio that is still alive and still selling. Nothing here is a rescue: this is an ownership story, and the upgrade path is one click away from every classic page that points at it.
