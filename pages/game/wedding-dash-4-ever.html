ABOUT WEDDING DASH 4-EVER

Wedding Dash 4-Ever, released on 10 august 2010, is the fourth and final PC entry of the spin-off line, developed and published by PlayFirst. The campaign runs as ten themed seasons of weddings, each batch of venues pushing the guest mix and the crisis pacing further than the last.

It keeps the Ready, Aim, Love set pieces and folds in the era's usual upgrades between weddings, and it shipped at the exact moment the casual-download model that carried the whole Dash franchise started to move on. The title turned out to be a promise the PC line couldn't keep - nothing in the series followed it on windows.

PlayFirst folded into glu mobile in 2014 and the download channels died with it, leaving the 2010 build un-buyable and preserved through archives only.
