ABOUT KINGDOMS AND CASTLES

Kingdoms and castles is a city builder with a medieval core: raise a town from a keep and a granary into walled districts, balance food chains against viking raids and winters, and grow the skyline in a tax-and-happiness economy that reads clearly at a glance. The building aesthetic is stylized-low-poly, which keeps the grid readable as the town scales.

Lion shield shipped it in july 2017 after a kickstarter, and the steam listing is the current official door with the active update line. It still sells, so the page is an official-route entry.

It is a light 3d builder - the haswell igpu bar holds it at 1080p with settings to spare. Not-tested stands until a run confirms install, launch and saves.
