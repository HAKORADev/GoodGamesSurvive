ABOUT SOAP OPERA DASH

Soap Opera Dash moves the chained-service loop out of the diner and onto a television studio floor: a soap opera is filming in dinertown, and the dash now feeds a cast and crew instead of hungry customers - props, wardrobe, makeup and the shoot itself all run through the same seat-them, serve-them rhythm, with diva moods standing in for diner patience meters.

PlayFirst published it in 2008 on windows, and the dinertown skin is unmistakable - the diner dash wiki lists it on the dash-series shelf next to the other side lines, right between the seasonal packs and the town's tycoon detour. The year is carried here per the hosted windows build; the game's own era is the late-portal window when playfirst was spinning the formula into every workplace it could think of.

It never reached steam and its store died with the portal. MyAbandonware hosts the windows build at 63 MB, which makes the route table short and honest: one archive door, no official one.
