ABOUT AUTO MODELLISTA

Auto modellista (2002) is capcom's first racing game: cel-shaded art over genuine tuning depth, drift-heavy arcade physics and an online lobby that was ahead of its console's playerbase. The owner's cel-shaded note describes exactly what makes it memorable - the comic look over honest drift tuning - and the style remains the point two decades later.
