ABOUT HYDRO THUNDER

Hydro thunder (1999) is the arcade powerboat racer that made water physics fun by cheating brilliantly: nine over-the-top boats, hidden shortcuts through waterfalls and canyons, a boost meter with nine pulses, and tracks that run the nile, arctic circles and shipwreck graveyards. The pc port carried the whole arcade - every boat, every shortcut - and the hull-bump physics still feel right.

Midway's collapse put the game wherever arcade licenses go to sleep; the archive route keeps the throttles open.
