ABOUT BEYOND GOOD & EVIL

Beyond Good & Evil puts you in jade's boots - a photojournalist on the mining colony of Hillys who investigates the alpha sections' disappearances with a camera, a hovercraft and a crew of hybrids. The loop mixes zelda-style dungeons, photography puzzles, racing and stealth, and its 2003 story about manufactured truth is the reason the title never left the conversation.

Ubisoft montpellier's windows port reached steam in may 2008. The listing stands today, but the purchase door is closed - ubisoft pulled the buy option as the franchise pivoted to the 2024 twentieth-anniversary edition, so the original pc build now sits in the un-buyable lane with the store page as its headstone.

The port is a 2008 build of a 2003 console game - single-digit-GB territory on the estimate ladder, and playable settings well under the haswell igpu bar. No archive route is attached yet; the slot stays empty until a real one turns up.
