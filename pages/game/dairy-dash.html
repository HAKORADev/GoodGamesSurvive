ABOUT DAIRY DASH

Dairy Dash, released for windows on 23 may 2008, takes the chained-service loop out of the diner and onto a farm. Animals queue instead of customers: cows need milking, chickens need feeding, fields need watering, and every chore competes for the same two hands.

The farm setting trades the seating chart for a chore chart, but the underlying design - overlapping tasks, patience curves, chain bonuses - is the diner engine wearing overalls. It shipped through playfirst's channel in the side line's 2008 wave.

No official channel survives the publisher's collapse into glu mobile, so the 2008 build is an un-buyable archive case.
