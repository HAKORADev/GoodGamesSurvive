ABOUT HEAVY WEAPON

Heavy Weapon deluxe is popcap's side-scrolling shooter: a rolling tank fights through cold-war-flavored stages, collecting weapon upgrades from flak to homing rockets while dodging artillery from every lane. Each stage ends in a boss tank with named weak points, and the megalaser assembly is the run's long-game goal.

Popcap shipped the deluxe windows build in 2006, and it still lists on steam under the popcap label at a live price - another of the studio's surviving originals on the official ladder.
