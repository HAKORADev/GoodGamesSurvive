ABOUT GARDEN DASH

Garden Dash, released for windows in mid-april 2011, is the final game of the DinerTown side line. The plot is a community garden: beds need watering, weeds need pulling, produce needs delivering, and every chore chain competes for the same clock as the day's customers arrive.

The gardening verbs layer cleanly onto the side line's engine, and the campaign's neighbourhood-revival frame gives it the same rebuild-everything arc as the diner games. It shipped in the same period playfirst wound its pc line down, which is why the side line ends here.

No official channel survives the publisher's collapse, so the 2011 build is an un-buyable archive case.
