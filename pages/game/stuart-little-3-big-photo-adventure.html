ABOUT STUART LITTLE 3: BIG PHOTO ADVENTURE

Stuart little 3: big photo adventure (2005) adapts the direct-to-video third film into a kid-sized platformer: stuart photographs the lake trail wildlife, rides bugs and completes fetch loops sized for the audience. It is exactly what it says on the box - a gentle collect-a-thon tie-in - and it closes the film trilogy's game line.
