ABOUT BROTATO

Brotato is the arena roguelite where a potato with up to six weapons survives escalating waves of aliens. The loop is tight by design: each wave runs under a minute-ish timer, materials double as xp and shop currency, and the build math - six weapon slots, stat re-rolls, item synergies - decides whether wave twenty ends in a boss kill or a swiss-cheese potato.

Blobfish shipped it in 2023 and the steam listing is the current official door at full price with active updates. The pc build is the original platform for the game, not a port riding behind a mobile line.

It is a light 2d sprite-pixel game - the haswell igpu bar sits far above its needs, and the run state is honest: not-tested until install, launch and saves are confirmed end to end.
