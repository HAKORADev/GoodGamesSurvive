ABOUT SPLIT/SECOND: VELOCITY

Split/Second: velocity (2010) is black rock studio's demolition racer: drifting and drafting charge a powerplay meter, and the meter detonates the track itself - crumbling cooling towers, dropping bridges, a plane landing across the circuit. Season mode is structured reality-tv style, and the two-level powerplay system remains the genre's smartest track-interaction idea.

Disney delisted the game for years; the current steam listing restored the official door for the windows build, which keeps the track demolition playable legally.
