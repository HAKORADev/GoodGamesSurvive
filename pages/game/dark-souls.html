ABOUT DARK SOULS

Dark souls is the open-ended action rpg where the world itself is the tutorial: lordran's interlocking shortcuts, bonfire checkpoints and ambush design teach the stamina-combat loop that defined the fromsoftware era. Invaders and summons ride the same server, covenant choices re-shape the online rules, and the loop of dying, learning and re-routing is the game.

Fromsoftware shipped the original in 2011; the steam listing today is the remastered edition (qloc port, 2018) - the current official door, since the original 2011 prepare-to-die build was delisted. The page documents the line honestly: the remaster is what the store sells, and it is still sold.

The remaster runs at the haswell igpu bar for reduced settings and scales up cleanly. The honest state is not-tested until a run confirms install, launch and the save system's behavior.
