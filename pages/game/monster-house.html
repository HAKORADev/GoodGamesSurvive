ABOUT MONSTER HOUSE

Monster house (2006) turns the movie's premise into a three-character action adventure: dj, chowder and jenny infiltrate the possessed house in rotating stages, each kid with a different tool - water balloons, kite, digger - against the living floorboards. The level design respects the film's blueprints, which makes it one of thq's better tie-ins.
