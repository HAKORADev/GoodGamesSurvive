ABOUT BEN 10 ULTIMATE ALIEN: COSMIC DESTRUCTION

Ben 10 ultimate alien: cosmic destruction (2011) is the ps2's late-era ben 10 entry: the ultimate alien forms join the omnitrix rotation, the tour stretches from paris to tokyo, and the combat layers upgrades onto the familiar swap-mid-fight law. It is also one of the last licensed brawlers the console received, which gives it a small archival seat of its own.
