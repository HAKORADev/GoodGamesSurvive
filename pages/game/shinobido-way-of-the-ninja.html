ABOUT SHINOBIDO: WAY OF THE NINJA

Shinobido: way of the ninja (2005) is acquire's follow-up to tenchu's stealth law: an amnesiac ninja sells contract work to three feuding warlords, and every mission is a sandbox of rooftops, guards and honest line-of-sight stealth. The mission editor and the loyalty economy gave it replay depth the genre still rarely matches.
