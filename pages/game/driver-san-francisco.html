ABOUT DRIVER: SAN FRANCISCO

Driver: san francisco (2011) is the series' comeback built on one mechanic: the shift - tanner can jump between any car in the city mid-chase, turning pursuit into a chess game played with traffic. The 1969 dodge charger battle with jericho frames a hallucinated city, the car culture spans a hundred licensed machines, and the multiplayer wrote its own rules around the same shift law.

The windows build is famous for being un-buyable: licensing pulled it from every store, so the archive route is the only way back in. It remains the best-reviewed driver game ever made.
