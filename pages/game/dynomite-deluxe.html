ABOUT DYNOMITE DELUXE

Dynomite deluxe is popcap's take on the bust-a-move formula: a dinosaur-launched egg bounce-matches against a descending wall, with physics-driven bank shots doing the work straight shots cannot. The modes split into endless puzzle layers and a survival-ish streak mode, each graded by the same clean popcap scoring grammar.

Popcap shipped the deluxe windows build in 2006, and it still lists on steam under the popcap label with a price - another of the surviving originals from the studio's prime window. Official route, no archive needed.
