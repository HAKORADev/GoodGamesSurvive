ABOUT VAMPIRE SURVIVORS

Vampire survivors is the bullet-heaven simplification: steer, auto-attack, survive the wave clock, and watch the weapon-evolution tree turn garlic and whips into screen-clearing engines. The gold economy, the relic unlocks and the character roster give the thirty-minute runs a meta-structure that made it the genre's template.

Poncle shipped it in october 2022 after a viral early access, and the steam listing is the current official door at a low price with the dlc roster expanding. The windows build is the original platform.

A light 2d sprite game - the haswell igpu bar holds it comfortably even in thousand-enemy late waves. Not-tested stands until a run confirms install, launch and saves.
