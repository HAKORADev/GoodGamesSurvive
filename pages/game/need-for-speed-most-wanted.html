ABOUT NEED FOR SPEED: MOST WANTED

Need for Speed: Most Wanted (2012) is criterion's open-city reimagining: no lobbies, no menus - you drive, the map is the lobby, and every rival car becomes a roaming boss you call out and wreck. The burnout crash physics carry the pursuits, and autolog 2 turned every speed camera and road into a leaderboard argument between friends.

The windows build is still sold on steam as the current official door, which keeps the criterion era playable in the era of the classics being gone.
