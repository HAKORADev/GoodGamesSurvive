ABOUT CRYSIS 2

Crysis 2 moves the fight to new york: the nanosuit's armor, stealth and power modes drive both the gunplay and the route through a city occupied by the cell corporation and torn apart by cephalopod invaders. The campaign's set pieces run from flooded subway lines to central park's finale, and the suit's cloak-and-switch rhythm is the reason the game survives its era's shooter comparison.

Crytek shipped it in march 2011, and the steam listing is the maximum edition - the current official door carrying the base campaign with its content batch. It still sells, so the page documents an official route rather than an archive rescue.

The 2011 cryengine 3 build sits right at the haswell igpu bar: 720p-900p playable with reduced settings, scaling well above. Not-tested stands until a real run confirms install, launch and saves.
