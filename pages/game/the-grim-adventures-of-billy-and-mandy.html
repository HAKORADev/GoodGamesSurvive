ABOUT THE GRIM ADVENTURES OF BILLY & MANDY

The grim adventures of billy & mandy (2006) is midway's arena brawler for the cartoon: billy, mandy, grim and the endsville roster brawl in four-player arenas built from the show's locations, with hazard comedy doing the balancing. It is a party fighter in the smash-bros shape, and the show's art direction survives intact.

No windows build exists, so the platform is ps2 - played through pcsx2, rom sourced from romsfun.
