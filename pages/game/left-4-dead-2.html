ABOUT LEFT 4 DEAD 2

Left 4 dead 2 is the co-op shooter whose ai director still defines the genre: four survivors run panic-and-barricade campaigns while the director scripts the pressure - special infected spawns, item economy, crescendo events. Melee weapons, new special infected and realism modes layered on the 2008 formula, and the workshop's community campaigns keep the content flow alive.

Valve shipped it in november 2009, and the steam listing is the current official door at a token price with active maintenance. It remains one of the store's most-played co-op fixtures.
