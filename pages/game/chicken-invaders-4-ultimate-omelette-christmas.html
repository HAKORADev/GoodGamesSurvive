ABOUT CHICKEN INVADERS 4: ULTIMATE OMELETTE - CHRISTMAS EDITION

Ultimate Omelette Christmas Edition (24 November 2011) is the first of the fourth episode's three holiday variants, and the second Christmas edition of the series after The Next Wave's 2003 original. The full Ultimate Omelette campaign - Egg Cannon included - runs under the snow.

Editions of this episode were never bare reskins: the holiday builds carry their own wave dressing, their own pickup tables, and the kind of detail work that made collectors treat them as complete games. This one shipped from the studio's own channels rather than portals, and it never made the jump to Steam, which makes the official site the primary source for it to this day.

That is also why it sits in this archive's rescue logic: the only legitimate distribution is the developer's own download hub. If InterAction ever closes that door, the anyway links are what remain.
