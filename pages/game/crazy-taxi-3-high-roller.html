ABOUT CRAZY TAXI 3: HIGH ROLLER

Crazy taxi 3: high roller (2002) is the pc chapter of sega's fare-chasing arcade: small apple and west coast maps return remastered, glitter oasis joins as the night city, and the crazy jump system turns every park job into a stunt. The off-duty meter, the crazy drift and the original of orange beach-buggy physics complete the loop that made the series an arcade landmark.
