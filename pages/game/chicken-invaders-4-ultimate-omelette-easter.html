ABOUT CHICKEN INVADERS 4: ULTIMATE OMELETTE - EASTER EDITION

Ultimate Omelette Easter Edition (11 March 2012) leans into the one holiday the series was born to parody: a war against egg-laying invaders, celebrated with eggs. The full fourth-episode campaign runs in spring dressing, from the opening waves to the Egg Cannon itself.

By 2012 the edition line was a machine - Christmas, Easter and eventually Thanksgiving variants of the same engine, each with its own dressing and wave twists - and this is the middle child of the Ultimate Omelette trio. Like its siblings it shipped from the studio's own distribution, never Steam, so the official download hub remains the legitimate source.

It completes the Ultimate Omelette set on this archive's shelf: standard, Christmas, Easter, Thanksgiving - four builds of the fourth war, all playable on hardware far below the target bar.
