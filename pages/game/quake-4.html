ABOUT QUAKE 4

Quake 4 continues the strogg war from quake 2's ending: matthew kane's rhino squad fights planet-wide while the campaign's centerpiece - matthew's half-stroggification - re-tools the story into cyborg identity horror. The gunplay is id-tech 4 grounded: squad sections, vehicle rides, and the hardcore strogg arenas between them.

Raven and id shipped it in october 2005; the steam listing is the current official door, still sold. The pc build is the original platform, and the page documents it as such.

Id-tech 4 2005 sits comfortably at the haswell igpu bar for native-ish settings. Not-tested stands until a run confirms install, launch and saves.
