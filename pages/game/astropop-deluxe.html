ABOUT ASTROPOP DELUXE

Astropop deluxe is the forgotten gem of popcap's mid-decade run: blocks drift down a chamber, you grab any block into a queue and fire it back to build match-3 chains, and the twist is that the queue itself is a weapon - a full magazine triggers a screwin' blast that clears a whole column. Four pilots with different powers, bonus rounds and a survival mode keep the board from ever settling.

The steam listing is the deluxe build still sold under popcap's ea-era catalog, which makes this one of the few grab-and-blast puzzles of the era that never went dark. It runs on anything and the loop still feels sharper than most of what it inspired.
