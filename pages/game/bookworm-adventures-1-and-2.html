ABOUT BOOKWORM ADVENTURES 1 & 2

Bookworm Adventures turns spelling into combat: lex the bookworm fights through storybook chapters, and the longer the word you spell from his letter grid, the harder the hit. Gem tiles add burning, poison and freeze effects, treasures and pet companions round out a light rpg layer, and the enemies run from alice in wonderland's queen of hearts to greek mythology in the sequel's tour.

The row covers both pc chapters - bookworm adventures (2006) and its 2007 sequel bookworm adventures volume 2 - which popcap delisted everywhere after the merger era; the original web bookworm stays a different, smaller game. The fighting-words formula never got a real successor, which is exactly why the pair is worth keeping installed.
