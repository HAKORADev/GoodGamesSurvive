ABOUT CANDY CRUSH SAGA (APPX)

Candy crush saga is the match-3 that defined the 2010s: swap-to-match boards with six candy types, cascades that score by chain, and a difficulty wall built to sell lives. The windows store build is the same saga the phones ran, boards and all, and it remains the genre's most-played entry by a distance.

The store listing is free with the usual monetization, and store.rg-adguard mirrors the appx packages directly for offline installs.
