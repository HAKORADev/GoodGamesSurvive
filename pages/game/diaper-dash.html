ABOUT DIAPER DASH

Diaper Dash, released on 12 august 2009, puts the loop in a daycare where the customers are babies. Feeding, rocking, changing and playing follow the chained-service rhythm, and a crying infant spreads impatience through the room faster than any diner guest ever did.

The build shipped in the same playfirst steam batch as the other 2009 dash games - produced with publisher credit to zemnott on the box - and that steam listing still sells the game today.

It stays on the buyable side of the shelf, with the archive routes riding along for the original-era build.
