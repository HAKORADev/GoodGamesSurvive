ABOUT HITMAN HD TRILOGY

Hitman HD Trilogy is the 2013 console remaster of the middle three hitman games: Hitman 2: Silent Assassin, Hitman 3: Contracts and Hitman: Blood Money, rebuilt in hd on one disc and published by square enix on january 29, 2013 for playstation 3 and xbox 360. It was the first time the pre-absolution catalog got the remaster treatment, with trophies and achievements bolted onto the three campaigns.

The fact that matters most for a windows archive is the one the box art makes obvious: no pc version ever existed. The trilogy is a console-only release, so it never touched the windows line - the pc builds of all three games it remasters stayed the originals, and those originals are exactly what the archive digs on their own pages. This page exists to document the remaster line honestly, not to pretend a pc build is hiding somewhere.

The digital story ended in 2019: when the Hitman HD Enhanced Collection arrived for ps4 and xbox one, the 2013 trilogy was pulled from the console stores, leaving physical copies as the only route. The upgrade lines on the silent assassin, contracts and blood money pages point here with the console-only note - the remaster is real, it just never happened on pc.
