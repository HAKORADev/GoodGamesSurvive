ABOUT DONKEY KONG COUNTRY RETURNS

Donkey kong country returns (2010) is retro studios' revival: every level is a machine of barrel cannons, mine carts and rocket barrels tuned to nintendo's cruellest difficulty curve, with diddy's jetpack as the mercy rule. The two-player couch mode turns the difficulty into a comedy of grief, and the banana-hunt completionism gave the wii its best pure platformer.
