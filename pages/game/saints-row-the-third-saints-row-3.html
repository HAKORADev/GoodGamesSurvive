ABOUT SAINTS ROW: THE THIRD (SAINTS ROW 3)

Saints row: the third is the series' pivot into full absurdity: the saints are celebrities, steelport is the syndicate's city, and the missions run from a freefall gunfight onto a skyscraper to a tiger kept in an elevator. Upgrades slide from gang ops into tank-airstrike absurdity, and the tone shift defined the franchise's later identity.
