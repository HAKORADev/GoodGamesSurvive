ABOUT NARUTO SHIPPUDEN: ULTIMATE NINJA HEROES 3 & AKATSUKI RISING

This row carries the two psp naruto shippuden action games western players actually got. Ultimate ninja heroes 3 (2009) is the roster fighter: over fifty characters, 4-player ad-hoc battles and the series' support-character system. Akatsuki rising (2009) takes the same cast into a side-scrolling beat-em-up with akatsuki as playable unlockables - the closest the franchise came to a proper action brawler on handheld.
