ABOUT SAINTS ROW IV (SAINTS ROW 4)

Saints row iv drops the boss into a virtual steelport simulation: super speed, super jumps and telekinesis replace the cars-and-guns loop of the earlier rows, dubstep guns and shredder easter eggs ride the comedy, and keith david playing keith david is the tone in one sentence. The re-elected package carries all the dlc arcs including the gat out of hell setup.

Volition shipped it in august 2013; the steam listing is the re-elected edition - the current official door carrying the base campaign plus its content, still sold under deep silver.
