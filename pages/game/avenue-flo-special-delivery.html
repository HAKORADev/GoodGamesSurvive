ABOUT AVENUE FLO: SPECIAL DELIVERY

Avenue Flo: Special Delivery, the second and last Avenue Flo adventure, reached windows at the end of 2010. A baby shower turns into an emergency delivery, and Flo's errand run across DinerTown becomes a race against the due date, with the cast from the first adventure returning as neighbours rather than customers.

The structure holds: walkable town, inventory puzzles, hidden-object scenes and mini-games, no timers. It was also the last adventure build playfirst shipped for pc before the studio's move ended the line.

With the publisher folded into glu mobile and its store long dead, the 2010 build is un-buyable - preserved through archives and hosted originals only.
