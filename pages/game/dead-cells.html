ABOUT DEAD CELLS

Dead cells fuses roguelite structure with metroidvania routes: every run through the castle, sewers, ramps and clock tower re-rolls the map while the meta-progression - cells, permanent unlock trees, item pools - keeps growing. The combat is fast and commitment-based, with mutation builds, shields-as-parry weapons and cursed chests trading power for risk.

Motion twin shipped it in august 2018 after an early-access year, and the steam listing is the current official door with the full dlc roster attached. It still sells, keeping the page in the official lane.

It is a light 2d pixel-art game - the haswell igpu bar holds it at 1080p60 with headroom. Not-tested stands until a run confirms install, launch and saves.
