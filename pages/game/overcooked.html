ABOUT OVERCOOKED!

Overcooked is the couch co-op kitchen sim: orders queue while the crew chops, cooks, washes and plates across kitchens designed to fall apart - moving trucks, splitting islands, haunted bread. The local co-op focus is the point, and the campaign's star thresholds turn a full crew's panic into a scoring sport.

Ghost town games shipped it in august 2016, and the steam listing is the current official door, still sold with the local co-op flags the design demands. The gourmet edition bundles the dlc separately - the listing here is the base campaign package.

A light 3d game of modest weight - the haswell igpu bar holds it comfortably at 1080p60. Not-tested stands until a run confirms install, launch and the couch co-op path.
