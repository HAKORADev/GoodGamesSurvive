ABOUT CHICKEN INVADERS 5: CLUCK OF THE DARK SIDE - CHRISTMAS EDITION

Cluck of the Dark Side Christmas Edition (19 September 2016) is the most recent holiday build in the classic Chicken Invaders line - after it, the studio's attention moved to Universe, and the edition tradition paused with it.

It carries the complete fifth-episode campaign under the decorations: Henterprise's fifth attempt on Earth, the full weapon system, co-op from the mainline, all re-dressed for the season. The Christmas edition line now reads as a series history in itself - 2003, 2006, 2011, 2016 - one per era of the engine.

Bundled with the mainline on Steam per the series' edition policy, and still distributed from the studio's own hub. For the shelf, this is the classic line's last bow: the fifth war, fought in the snow, still running on hardware the target machine outruns.
