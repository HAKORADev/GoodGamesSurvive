ABOUT FITNESS DASH

Fitness Dash, released for windows on 6 november 2008, moves the loop into DinerTown's gym. Members queue with workout goals, equipment needs constant re-racking, and the trainer's day is the same overlapping-task puzzle the diner runs - the fitness variants change which muscle group the clock squeezes.

Machines, classes and locker-room chaos fill the station roster, and gym upgrades between levels follow the side line's renovation pattern. The build later reached steam in the playfirst batch and, unusually for the side line, that listing still sells it.

That keeps Fitness Dash on the buyable side of the shelf, with the archive routes riding along for the original-era builds.
