ABOUT JACKIE CHAN ADVENTURES

Jackie chan adventures (2001) adapts the animated series into a beat-em-up with the show's improvised-combat hook: grab ladders, chairs and anything else the room offers, chase the dark hand's talismans and let jade narrate. The environmental-weapon law matches the show's choreography better than its budget suggested.
