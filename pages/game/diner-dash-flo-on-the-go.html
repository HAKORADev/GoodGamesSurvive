ABOUT DINER DASH: FLO ON THE GO

Diner Dash: Flo on the Go is the third game in the line, published by PlayFirst in 2006. The twist is the venue: Flo and Darla take a vacation that turns into working passage, and the diner moves onto a cruise ship - then to train stations, plane cabins and other stops along the route, each with its own service rhythm.

The onboard twist adds luggage-handling and wardrobe changes between shifts, but the core loop stays pure Diner Dash: seat, serve, chain, bus, and keep the patience curves from colliding. The PC original ran as a small self-contained build, the kind of game that outlives the portals that sold it.

No official channel survives - PlayFirst folded into Glu Mobile in 2014 and the download portals died with it - so the 2006 PC build rides the archive route only.
