ABOUT ZUMA DELUXE

Zuma deluxe is the marble shooter that named the genre: a stone frog spits colored balls into a rolling chain, matches of three burst, gaps score, and the reverse-and-slow power balls keep the spirals survivable. The temple stages escalate from spiral basics to gap-shot mastery, and the genre wave it spawned - luxor, tumblebugs and the rest of this catalogue - traces straight back to it.

Popcap shipped the deluxe windows build in 2006 after the 2003 web original, and it still lists on steam under the popcap label with a live price - the classic's official door, standing.
