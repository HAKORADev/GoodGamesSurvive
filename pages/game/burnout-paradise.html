ABOUT BURNOUT PARADISE

Burnout paradise (2008 consoles, 2009 pc) rebuilt the crash-physics racer as an open city: events start at any traffic light, races route themselves across real streets, and every crash is a physics lesson. The ultimate box pc package carried the party mode, bikes and all the islands; the 2020 remastered door on steam keeps the whole city alive at modern resolutions.

The current official door is burnout paradise remastered - a straight rebuild of the same game, not a different one. The original ultimate box build is delisted.
