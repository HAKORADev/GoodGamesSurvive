ABOUT NEED FOR SPEED: UNDERGROUND 2

Need for Speed: Underground 2 (2004) is the customization peak of the series: an open bayview city of five districts, discovery-based event unlocks, and a visual editor that could consume whole evenings before a single race. Drift, drag, street x and url circuits cycle under the same car, and the suv and tuner catalogs remain unmatched by anything the franchise shipped after.

The windows build is delisted with the rest of the classic nfs line, so the archive route keeps bayview lit.
