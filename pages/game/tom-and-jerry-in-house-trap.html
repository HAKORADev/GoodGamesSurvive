ABOUT TOM AND JERRY IN HOUSE TRAP

Tom and jerry in house trap (2000) turns the cartoon's whole grammar into a trap-warfare brawler: tom and jerry raid the pantry setting spring-loaded traps on each other, two players make it a proper versus game, and the house's rooms are the levels. It is the most faithful tom-and-jerry game mechanics ever got.
