ABOUT BEN 10: PROTECTOR OF EARTH

Ben 10: protector of earth (2007) turns the omnitrix into the game's whole grammar: swap between heatblast, four arms, xlr8 and the rest mid-fight, each alien a different combat answer to the same alien-invasion problem. The beat-em-up loop is honest about its license and the show's voice cast carries it.
