ABOUT QBEEZ 2

Qbeez whirled (the row's second chapter) sends grinning cubes tumbling across isometric boards: click one group and the qbeez scatter in every direction, chaining into the kind of cascades that made early-2000s puzzle portals glow. Bonus rounds flip the board into whirled mini-games, and the difficulty comes from how little of the board you can afford to miss.

Skunk studios' catalog went dark with the portal era it grew up in, so the pc build survives on archive routes. The cascade math still holds up - few clearing puzzles since have been this generous with chain reactions.
