ABOUT TERRARIA

Terraria is the 2d sandbox survival game: dig through a procedurally layered world, build what the materials suggest, and fight a boss ladder whose tiers re-shape the world - the eye, the wall of flesh's hardmode pivot, plantera, the moon lord. The crafting tree is enormous, the building freedom is the quiet appeal, and re-logic's post-release update decade became its own legend.

Re-logic shipped it in may 2011, and the steam listing is the current official door at a low price with the 1.4 update line. The windows build is the original platform.

A light 2d game - the haswell igpu bar holds it at 1080p with huge headroom. Not-tested stands until a run confirms install, launch and saves.
