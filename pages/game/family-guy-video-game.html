ABOUT FAMILY GUY VIDEO GAME!

Family guy video game! (2006) splits the show's humor across three playable threads: stewie's platforming revenge on bertram, peter's bone-crushing beat-em-up of random cutaway violence, and brian's stealth against the evil monkey. The cutaway joke structure translates better than critics of the time admitted - the voice cast is the real cast, and the writing is the real writing.
