ABOUT HUNGRY SHARK (APPX / CLASSIC)

Hungry shark evolution is the growth-by-eating arcade: steer a shark through open water, eat everything smaller, dodge everything bigger, and watch the food chain turn into gold. The windows store build was a paid app before the store retired it, which puts it in the rare club of appx games you can no longer legally buy anywhere.

The owner-provided archive zip carries the complete install set for the store build - unzip it and the game installs as designed. It is the only appx page in this catalogue that skips the store entirely, and honestly so.
