ABOUT CHICKEN INVADERS 4: ULTIMATE OMELETTE - THANKSGIVING EDITION

Ultimate Omelette Thanksgiving Edition (15 November 2013) is a one-of-a-kind in the Chicken Invaders line: the only Thanksgiving edition the series ever produced. The turkeys join the chickens, the dressing goes full harvest festival, and the fourth war gets its strangest costume.

By the game's own tagline the turkeys 'revolted three years ago' - a joke at the expense of the edition's own release calendar, since it shipped three years after the standard episode. Under the feathers it is the complete Ultimate Omelette campaign, same engine, same co-op, same Egg Cannon.

It is also the rarest of the four builds to find discussed anywhere, which is exactly the kind of item this archive exists for: a real release, still distributed by its own studio, that most people never learned existed.
