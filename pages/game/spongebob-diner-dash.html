ABOUT SPONGEBOB SQUAREPANTS DINER DASH

SpongeBob SquarePants Diner Dash, released on 11 may 2006, is the Diner Dash formula under the nickelodeon license, published by playfirst. SpongeBob takes the waiter role, the Krusty Krab and its rivals become the venues, and the customer cast swaps DinerTown regulars for Bikini Bottom regulars with their own patience quirks.

The service chain is untouched - seat, serve, bus, chain - which is exactly what made the crossover work: the license changes the skin, the five-minute engine stays. It was the first time the formula left DinerTown, and the only time it carried a cartoon license on pc.

The license window closed and the download channels died with the publisher, so the 2006 build is un-buyable and preserved through archives only.
