ABOUT HITMAN: CODENAME 47

Hitman: Codename 47 (November 2000) is the origin: IO Interactive's first game, and the debut of the bald barcode at the back of a genre's neck. Built around a simple, radical idea for its time - you are the assassin, and the game scores you on how invisible the murder was, not how loud - it invented the sandbox-assassination loop that everything after it refines.

The story is the franchise's foundation stone: a genetically engineered clone, grown by Dr. Ort-Meyer from the combined stock of four men - Lee Hong, Pablo Ochoa, Frantz Fuchs, Arkadij Jegorov - escaping the asylum that made him and working his way down a contract list that quietly leads home. Diana Burnwood, the ICA handler on the other end of the earpiece, appears here for the first time and never leaves.

It is a hard, stiff product of 2000 - no save-scumming comforts, demanding disguise logic, checkpoints that respect nothing - and it remains the only mainline entry that was never console-native. GOG's build runs it on Windows 10/11, which is exactly the target machine's shape. The upcoming Classic Trilogy Remastered (Saber + IOI) will rebuild it; until that lands, this is the source.
