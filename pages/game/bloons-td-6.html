ABOUT BLOONS TD 6

Bloons TD 6 is the modern peak of the tower-defense formula: monkey towers across dart, magic, military, support and ice classes, each with three five-tier upgrade paths, fighting balloon waves that escalate from reds to boss-grade blimps. The maps layer line-of-sight and elevation into placement math, and co-op play turns the whole thing into a shared board.

Ninja kiwi shipped it in december 2018 and the windows build on steam is the current, actively updated version - the same build the mobile line rides. The steam door stays live with a price, so this page is an official-route entry, not an archive rescue.

It is a 3d-rendered but light tactical game - the haswell igpu bar holds it at playable settings, and the honest state is not-tested until a run confirms the install and the co-op pathing.
