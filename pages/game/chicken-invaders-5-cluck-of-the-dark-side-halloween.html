ABOUT CHICKEN INVADERS 5: CLUCK OF THE DARK SIDE - HALLOWEEN EDITION

Cluck of the Dark Side Halloween Edition (2 October 2015) is the first edition of the fifth episode and the series' first Halloween variant: the fifth invasion restaged as a horror night, with the Dark Side's corporate war machine glowing in the dark.

The full fifth-episode campaign runs underneath - Henterprise, the weapon cores, the co-op - with the holiday's dressing over the wave design. It shipped directly to Steam a month before the season, bundled with the mainline game per the series' edition policy, and it remains the newest Halloween content the classic line ever received.

As the only Halloween entry in the whole franchise it is a natural collector's target, and it completes this archive's Cluck of the Dark Side trio: standard, Halloween, Christmas.
