ABOUT RAYMAN ORIGINS

Rayman origins brought the limbless hero back as a hand-drawn 2d platformer: ubisoft montpellier's ubiart engine renders every sprite by hand, the levels swing from jungle trunks to musical land stages that play themselves to the beat, and the unlock curve keeps movement upgrades - sprint, hover, dive - arriving at a steady clip. Four-player couch co-op rides the same levels.

Ubisoft montpellier shipped it in november 2011; the steam listing (march 2012 windows build) is the current official door, still sold. The windows build is the original platform alongside consoles.

Ubiart is a light 2d engine - the haswell igpu bar holds 1080p60 with headroom. Not-tested stands until a run confirms install, launch and saves.
