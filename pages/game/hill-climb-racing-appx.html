ABOUT HILL CLIMB RACING (APPX)

Hill climb racing is the two-pedal physics runner: gas and brake, undulating hills, and a driver whose neck snaps if the landing goes wrong. Coins buy vehicles and stage unlocks, upgrades genuinely change the physics, and the loop is honest about what it is - the best five-minute drive in the windows store.

The store listing is free, and store.rg-adguard mirrors the appx package for offline installs.
