ABOUT PORTAL 2

Portal 2 expands the portal grammar into a full campaign: propulsion and repulsion gels, light bridges, funnels, cave johnson's aperture history tapes, and wheatley's mid-game chaos. The dedicated two-bot co-op campaign runs its own chamber set with ping-markered teamwork, and the writing kept the franchise's comedy bar absurdly high.

Valve shipped it in april 2011, and the steam listing is the current official door at a token price with the perpetual testing initiative's workshop ecosystem attached. Valve's own platform, still maintained.

Source 2011 sits under the haswell igpu bar at native settings. Not-tested stands until a run confirms install, launch and the co-op path.
