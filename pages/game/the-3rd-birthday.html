ABOUT THE 3RD BIRTHDAY

The 3rd birthday (2010) is the parasite eve spin-off that traded survival horror for third-person shooting with a twist: aya brea projects her soul into soldiers on the field, which doubles as the game's respawn, its puzzle tool and its narrative device. The time-collapsed manhattan setting gives the shooting a mood the series never revisited.
