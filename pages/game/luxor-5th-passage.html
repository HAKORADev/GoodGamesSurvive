ABOUT LUXOR 5TH PASSAGE

Luxor: 5th Passage (2010) is the series' send-off: a track pack that remixes the best stages of the line with a handful of new mechanics and the usual power-ball toolbox. It plays as both encore and argument that the formula never needed saving - the chains still roll, the gap shot still decides everything.
