ABOUT PIRATES OF THE CARIBBEAN: THE LEGEND OF JACK SPARROW

The legend of jack sparrow (2006) retells the first two pirates films as a stylized co-op brawler: jack narrates his own legend, each retelling grows more exaggerated, and will or elizabeth join as the second character. Eurocom's combat is honest about being a couch game first, which is where it lands its charm.
