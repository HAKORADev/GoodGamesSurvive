ABOUT PLAYBOY: THE MANSION

Playboy: the mansion (2005) is cyberlore's management sim with a Licensed smile: run the mansion, schedule the parties, court celebrities and journalists, and ship the magazine issue after issue. The adult content is mid-2000s cable-grade - the wall is the theme and the topless editor art - and the sim systems underneath are a genuine heirs-to-the-sims experiment that never got a sequel.
