ABOUT KINGDOM HEARTS II

Kingdom hearts ii (2005) opens with roxas' week in twilight town and then hands sora the deepest combat kit the series ever shipped: drive forms, reaction commands, summon teams and the most legible boss design square enix put on the ps2. The disney worlds are bigger, the org xiii arc closes cleanly, and the game remains the fan-canon peak of the franchise.
