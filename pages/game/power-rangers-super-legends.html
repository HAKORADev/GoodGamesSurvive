ABOUT POWER RANGERS: SUPER LEGENDS

Power rangers: super legends (2007) is the franchise's fifteenth-anniversary crossover: play through eras of the show - mighty morphin through operation overdrive - as rangers pulled across time to fight a shared threat. The beat-em-up loop is straightforward, the roster is the whole point, and the co-op makes it a proper couch brawler.
