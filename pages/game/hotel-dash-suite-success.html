ABOUT HOTEL DASH: SUITE SUCCESS

Hotel Dash: Suite Success, released for windows on 24 november 2009, moves Flo from the dining room to the front desk. Guests arrive with luggage, demands and room-service orders, and the chain now runs through elevators, hallways and housekeeping carts instead of tables.

The formula translates cleanly - patience curves, chaining and expert scores survive the hotel setting - while the new verbs (checking in, delivering luggage, restocking towels) give the loop a second axis. A boutique-hotel renovation layer between levels carries the campaign forward.

The build reached steam in the playfirst batch and still sells there today, which keeps it on the buyable side of the shelf; the archive routes ride along for the original-era builds.
