ABOUT LUXOR 2

Luxor 2 (2006) rebuilt the marble line in polished pseudo-3d: tracks curve through the frame instead of running flat, chains split and merge, and the power-ball pool grew into a genuine toolkit. It is the chapter that modernized the formula without bending its laws - and the one that most directly competed with zuma's revenge for the genre crown.
