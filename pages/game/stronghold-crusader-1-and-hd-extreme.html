ABOUT STRONGHOLD CRUSADER 1 (AND HD / EXTREME)

Stronghold crusader hd is firefly's desert-era castle game: the economy sim of the original stronghold - food chains, taxes, popularity - married to skirmish-style crusader lord matches across arabian maps. The unit roster trades the original's european flavor for camel archers, slingers and assassin stealth, and the ai lord roster remains the game's long-game.

Firefly shipped the original in 2002; the hd edition (2012-era refresh) is the steam listing - the current official door, still sold. The hd remaster is the pc line's living build; the 2002 original rides beneath it.
