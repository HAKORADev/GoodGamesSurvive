ABOUT CHICKEN INVADERS

Chicken Invaders is where the whole thing started: a 1999 fixed shooter by Konstantinos Prouskas of InterAction studios, openly built as a parody of Space Invaders and Galaxian. The premise never pretends to be anything else - intelligent chickens, armed and organized, are invading Earth in revenge for centuries of poultry oppression, and one lone spacecraft stands between them and the omelet end of history.

The game plays it straight as a fixed shooter: your ship slides along the bottom of the screen, waves of chickens descend in formations, and every fallen bird drops eggs, drumsticks and gift boxes that upgrade your weapon. What made it last is the comedy of scale - boss fights against enormous egg-laying war machines, waves that spell things out in chickens, and difficulty that keeps climbing long after the joke has landed.

It spawned five numbered sequels, a string of holiday editions, an MMO-styled spin-off, and its own 2023 remaster. The original is still handed out free by the studio that made it, which makes this one of the cleanest rescue stories in the archive: nothing was ever really lost.
