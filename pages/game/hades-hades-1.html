ABOUT HADES (HADES 1)

Hades is the roguelike where death is the story: zagreus fights out of the underworld again and again while the house of hades reacts to every escape attempt - relationships, boons from olympus, and weapon aspects that re-write each run's build. The isometric combat is fast and readable, the mirror of night anchors the meta-progression, and the writing made it the genre's story benchmark.

Supergiant shipped it in september 2020 after early access, and the steam listing is the current official door with the full 1.0 content. It still sells, keeping the page in the official lane.

It is a light 3d isometric game with clean art - the haswell igpu bar holds it at 1080p60 comfortably. Not-tested stands until a run confirms install, launch and saves.
