ABOUT RE-VOLT

Re-volt (1999) races remote-control cars through kitchens, gardens, museums and supermarkets with honest toy-scale physics: bouncy, twitchy, momentum-driven, and more demanding than its box art ever admitted. Twenty-eight cars across four classes, track-editor support and a pickup arsenal kept the community modding it for two decades after acclaim died.

The current steam listing is the restored official door for the windows build - one of the few 1999 racers that never actually left.
