ABOUT CHICKEN INVADERS 3: REVENGE OF THE YOLK - EASTER EDITION

Revenge of the Yolk Easter Edition (12 February 2010) is the series' first Easter variant, and per the series' own lore the episode that started the Easter tradition: eggs were already the enemy's ammunition, so the holiday only made things worse.

The body is the third episode's full campaign - the Yolk-Star, the 3D wave design, the co-op - dressed in spring colors with egg-themed pickups and holiday waves woven into the run. It shipped long after the standard edition, when the edition line was already a yearly ritual, and it completes the Revenge of the Yolk trio: Christmas, standard, Easter.

The Steam listing (2015) carries the remastered build of this edition. The original remains in the studio's own distribution, which keeps this one a clean ownership story.
