ABOUT INSANIQUARIUM DELUXE

Insaniquarium deluxe is the popcap oddity that preceded the studio's biggest hits: feed guppies, collect coins, buy pets whose passive powers re-shape each tank, and fight the alien waves that crash the aquarium on a timer. The economy loop is pure popcap - feed, earn, upgrade, defend - and the pet roster is the game's build-craft layer.

Popcap shipped the deluxe windows build in 2006 after its 2001 web origins, and it still lists on steam with a live price under the popcap label. Official route, archive not needed.
