ABOUT GRAND THEFT AUTO: SAN ANDREAS & VICE CITY

This row is the classic pc pairing of two landmark open worlds. Grand Theft Auto: Vice City (2002) drops tommy vercetti into a neon-soaked 1986 pastiche of miami - cocaine deals, chainsaws, a property empire and the best radio lineup the series ever cut. Grand Theft Auto: San Andreas (2004) follows carl johnson across three cities and the countryside between them in 1992 - the biggest map of its era, with role-playing layers the series never fully repeated: hunger, muscle, respect, territories.

Both classics are gone from digital sale since rockstar retired them for the definitive-edition remasters, so on pc the original builds live on through archive routes only - which is exactly what this page keeps. The trilogy definitive edition is the current official door for the content, but it is a different engine on different code, and the originals remain the versions this archive documents.
