ABOUT TOMB RAIDER: UNDERWORLD

Tomb raider: underworld closes crystal dynamics' lara trilogy: thors' gauntlets and hammer power the traversal, the mediterranean-to-mexico-to-niflheim run ties the trilogy's myth arc off, and the free-climbing, grapple-swing grammar is the trilogy's maturest form. The mystery of amelia and the hammer of thor give the campaign its spine.

Crystal dynamics shipped it in november 2008, and the steam listing is the current official door at a discount-tier price, still sold. The windows build is the original platform.
