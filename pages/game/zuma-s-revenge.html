ABOUT ZUMA'S REVENGE!

Zuma's revenge! is the direct sequel: the frog hops between lily pads now, the stage geometry opens into multi-angle arenas, and power-ups - tri-shot, laser, slow - arrive faster. The tiki-boss duels give the island structure a finale ladder, and the core chain-burst loop stays untouched.

Popcap shipped it in september 2009, and it still lists on steam under the popcap label with a live price. Official route, standing beside the original.
