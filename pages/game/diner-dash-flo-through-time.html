ABOUT DINER DASH: FLO THROUGH TIME

Diner Dash: Flo Through Time, released on 10 november 2008, is the expansion pack built on Diner Dash: Hometown Hero rather than a clean fifth sequel - it installs on the same engine and sends Flo's diners through the ages: a caveman cafe, a medieval banquet hall, and onward to a futurist diner.

The service loop is unchanged; the eras change the customers, the props and the hazard pacing, and the retail box was distributed through big fish games alongside playfirst's own channel. It is the only entry in the numbered line that is an expansion first - which is exactly why the shelf files it as part four-and-a-half.

Both of its parents are delisted - the expansion shipped through channels that no longer exist - so the 2008 build is an un-buyable archive case.
