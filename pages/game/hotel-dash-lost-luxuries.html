ABOUT HOTEL DASH: LOST LUXURIES

Hotel Dash: Lost Luxuries, the second Hotel Dash, reached windows in mid-2011 as playfirst's pc line was winding down - it was among the last Dash games the publisher produced for the platform. The setting upgrades from boutique hotels to a full luxury resort, and the missing amenities become the level structure: every restored spa, pool or concierge desk adds a station Flo has to cover.

The service chain from Suite Success carries over with more simultaneous demands, and the resort's rebuilding frame gives the campaign its shape. It shipped through the same download channels that folded with the publisher three years later.

No official channel survives - the 2011 build is un-buyable and lives on through archives and hosted originals only.
