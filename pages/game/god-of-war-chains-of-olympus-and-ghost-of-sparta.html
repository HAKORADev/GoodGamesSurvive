ABOUT GOD OF WAR: CHAINS OF OLYMPUS & GHOST OF SPARTA

This row carries ready at dawn's two psp god of war chapters, both technical miracles on the handheld. Chains of olympus (2008) sends kratos serving the gods before the first game - persian armies, the sun chariot, the story that explains his chains. Ghost of sparta (2010) follows his spartan past and his brother deimos, with the most graphically ambitious build the psp ever shipped.

No windows builds exist, so the platform is psp - played through ppsspp, roms sourced from romsfun.
