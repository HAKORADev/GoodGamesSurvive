ABOUT CHICKEN INVADERS 2: THE NEXT WAVE REMASTERED

Chicken Invaders 2: The Next Wave Remastered is the 2012 rebuild of the second war, and it is the oldest remaster in the series. The Christmas Edition actually got its rebuild first - 20 December 2011 - with the standard remaster following on 26 January 2012. The upgrade list from the studio reads: hi-res graphics, orchestral music, an automatic fire function, progress saving and new languages.

It stays on sale as the studio's current build of the second war: the interactionstudios.com product page for The Next Wave sells the remastered edition directly. The untouched 2002 original keeps its own page here as the archive truth of the classic build, holiday edition included.

The second war also rides the newer episodic rework (episode 2, shipped through the studio's free Episodes update since 2025) - that path has its own page. This remaster is the classic-engine route: same war, same waves, presented the way the studio modernized it in 2012.
