ABOUT DINERTOWN DETECTIVE AGENCY

DinerTown Detective Agency is the dinertown universe's detective spinoff - instead of chaining tables, you run the town's detective agency, working one case at a time across the same neighborhood the dash games built. The play shifts from time management to the hidden-object wave of the era: first-person scenes, item hunts, puzzle threads that stitch each case together.

PlayFirst published it in 2009 with a worldwide rollout that ran into 2010, and the development credit goes to Absolutist Ltd. per the archived release metadata - a studio better known for casual puzzle work, which shows in how cleanly the hidden-object scenes slot into the dinertown skin. It is the one entry in the universe that left the restaurant floor entirely while keeping the town itself as the cast list.

The channel that sold it died with playfirst's portal collapse, and steam never carried it. The windows build survives through the archive - myabandonware hosts it - and that is the whole route table: no store, no key server, preserved anyway.
