ABOUT CHICKEN INVADERS 4: ULTIMATE OMELETTE

Chicken Invaders 4: Ultimate Omelette (6 December 2010) is the biggest of the pre-Universe episodes and the one that gave the series its most absurd weapon of mass destruction: the Egg Cannon, a chicken-built superweapon aimed at Earth with the stated goal of frying the planet into a cosmic omelet.

It is the fourth war and the fourth engine iteration: sharper 3D, bigger wave variety, more satellites and weapon cores, and the series' most generous edition output - Ultimate Omelette is the only episode to receive a Thanksgiving Edition alongside Christmas and Easter variants. Co-op remains the center of gravity: four players, one screen, and an escalating storm of poultry.

The campaign structure keeps the episode formula - chapters of ten waves, boss confrontations, unlockable difficulty loops - while the presentation finally catches up to the joke's ambition. Among casual-shooter historians this is the peak of the classic line, and it still runs on integrated graphics that predate the target machine.
