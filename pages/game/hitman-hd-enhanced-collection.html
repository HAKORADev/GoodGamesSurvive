ABOUT HITMAN HD ENHANCED COLLECTION

Hitman HD Enhanced Collection, released january 11, 2019, is the second console remaster line: Hitman: Blood Money and Hitman: Absolution rebuilt at 4k resolution with 60 frames per second targets for playstation 4 and xbox one, published in the io-interactive era after the studio split from square enix. Both campaigns arrived with updated lighting, upscale support and the full achievement tracks.

The windows law holds here exactly as it does for the 2013 trilogy: the collection is a console-only product, and no pc build of it exists. Blood money and absolution on pc remain the 2006 and 2012 originals - both still sold - and those originals are what this archive digs. The collection's page documents the remaster line from the console side; the pc story stays with the pc games.

It also replaced its predecessor: the 2013 Hitman HD Trilogy came off the console stores once this collection took the shelf, which is why the trilogy's page reads as delisted. The upgrade lines on the blood money and absolution pages point here with the console-only note - on pc, the originals are still the real thing.
