ABOUT PEGGLE

Peggle is popcap's pachinko-cum-marble-popper: clear the orange pegs with a bouncing ball, ten peggle masters each bending the physics with their power, and the free-ball bucket at the bottom turning luck into streaks. Extreme fever, the ode to joy blast and the last-ball slow-motion are the franchise's signature drama.

Popcap shipped peggle deluxe in 2007 and it still lists on steam under the popcap label with a live price - one of the surviving originals from the studio's prime. Official route, archive not needed.
