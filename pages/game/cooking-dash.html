ABOUT COOKING DASH

Cooking Dash, released for windows in 2008 with development by Aliasworlds Entertainment under the PlayFirst banner, changes the protagonist's job rather than the protagonist: Flo drops the trays and takes the stove, cooking every order herself while Grandma runs the seating.

The design consequence is real - the chain now runs through preparation stations, so a wrong sequencing decision at the grill costs more than a wrong seating choice ever did. Multi-course meals, celebrity customers and kitchen upgrades carry the fifty-level campaign, and two players can still share one pc.

The build reached steam on 12 august 2009 as part of playfirst's store batch and that listing still sells it - one of the few official doors left into the Dash universe.
