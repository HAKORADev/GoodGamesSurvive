ABOUT MASS EFFECT (MASS EFFECT 1 / COLLECTION)

Mass effect is the origin of the trilogy: commander shepard's spectre investigation, the geth and sovereign plot, and a dialogue system whose paragon-renegade spectrum became bioWare's template for the decade. The citadel hub, the normandy crew and the mako's planetary bounces define its structure, and the lore codex made the galaxy feel bigger than its disc count.

BioWare shipped it in november 2007 for windows in 2008; the steam listing today is the 2007-labeled release under ea's catalog - the current official door for the original pc build, since the legendary edition remakes the trilogy separately. It still sells.

The unreal 3 build sits at the haswell igpu bar for reduced settings and scales above it. Not-tested stands until a run confirms install, launch and saves.
