ABOUT HITMAN 2 (2018)

HITMAN 2 (13 November 2018) is the trilogy's middle act and the first release after IOI went independent - self-published, no Square Enix, and nobody at the door to stop them. The Shadow Client from the 2016 finale steps into the open as Lucas Grey, an ally with a claim on 47's past, and the season sends the pair across Miami's racetrack, Santa Fortuna's Colombian village, Mumbai's slums, Vermont's suburb, the Isle of Sgail and New York's bank.

The sandbox craft deepens rather than changes: bigger crowds, permanent shortcuts unlocked by mastery, briefcases that made disguise-swap logistics an actual playstyle, and the introduction of Sniper Assassin co-op as a side mode. Arthur Edwards - Providence's Constant - becomes the trilogy's true antagonist over the season, and the ending hands 47 his biggest reveal about himself.

The standalone release was delisted in the January 2023 World of Assassination restructure; its levels now live inside WoA as part of the merged trilogy. Sixty gigabytes and a GTX 660 floor keep it off the target machine, so this page is documentation and ownership routing, not an archive target: the anyway link goes to where the content legally runs.
