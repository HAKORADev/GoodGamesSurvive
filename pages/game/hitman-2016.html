ABOUT HITMAN (2016)

HITMAN (2016) is the reboot that saved the series. After Absolution's linear detour, IOI rebuilt the sandbox dream at full scale and shipped it episodically through the year: Paris' fashion-show palace, Sapienza's entire coastal town, Marrakesh's souk, Colorado's militia farm, Hokkaido's hospital GARDEN. Every level is a self-contained world of routines, disguises and opportunities, and the craft got so clean that the game became the genre's template overnight.

The season's plot is a prologue in disguise: 47 and Diana's first meeting (playable as the ICA recruitment missions), a chain of contracts dismantling the shadow network IAGO, and the first glimpses of Providence - the secret government of the world that the whole trilogy chases. Erich Soders, the ICA board's hardliner, anchors the Hokkaido finale.

Ownership history matters here: as of 26 January 2023 the standalone release is delisted and its content lives inside HITMAN World of Assassination (2021's Hitman 3, rebranded). This page documents the 2016 season as a distinct release; the anyway links go where the content legally lives now. The engine wants a GTX 660 floor and 50GB of disk - far above the target machine, which is why the modern trilogy is catalogued as the series' upgrade path, not an archive target.
