ABOUT GRAND THEFT AUTO V

Grand Theft Auto V rotates three protagonists through a satirical los santos: michael, the retired bank robber with a witness-protection mansion; franklin, the repo man climbing toward real scores; trevor, the unfiltered desert lunatic. Heists are planned two ways - loud or smart - and the open world between them carries yoga, stock markets, stunt planes and the internet's worst corners. The switch-on-the-fly structure turned every mission into a small heist film.

On pc the game landed in april 2015, a year and a half after consoles, with a replay director, a first-person mode added later, and gta online - the persistent multiplayer city that carried the game through the decade. The current steam door is grand theft auto v enhanced, the 2025 rebuild; the legacy build stays the one this archive's older machines care about.
