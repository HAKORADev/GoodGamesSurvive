ABOUT MANHUNT 2

Manhunt 2 (2007) is the sequel rockstar could barely release: the execution-stealth law of the first game returns with daniel lamb's split psyche - and leo kasper's - driving the levels. The ratings war defined its history: banned in several countries, AO-rated in the us, released censored on consoles, with the windows build cancelled outright. The gore wall is the game's whole identity, documented here as exactly that.

No windows version ever shipped, so the platform is ps2 - the version this archive digs, played through pcsx2. The rom source is romsfun; the emulator and its setup live on the software shelf.
