ABOUT TES V: SKYRIM

The elder scrolls v: skyrim is the open-world rpg benchmark: the dovahkiin's shout system, the dragon encounters that re-shaped its genre's combat, and a province whose guild lines, civil war and daedric quests filled a decade of playthroughs. The perk-based leveling, the shout walls and the radiant quest engine define its structure.

Bethesda game studios shipped it in november 2011, and the steam listing is the current official door - the special-edition-line build with the anniversary content tiered above it. It still sells in every edition form.

The special edition's 64-bit build is mid-weight - the haswell igpu bar holds reduced settings with 1080p above. Not-tested stands until a run confirms install, launch and saves.
