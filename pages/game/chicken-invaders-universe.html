ABOUT CHICKEN INVADERS UNIVERSE

Chicken Invaders Universe is the series' MMO-styled successor - part six of the line, and the point where the lone spacecraft of the classic wars opens into a persistent online galaxy. You create your own pilot instead of stepping into the Hero's cockpit, fly missions across a living sector map, and drop into co-op with other players per the Steam online co-op flags. The core joke survives the transition intact: chickens, organized, in space, and you against them.

The road to 1.0 was long and public. A standalone beta ran from 2018, reachable straight from the studio. Steam early access opened on 16 April 2021, and the full 1.0 release landed on 15 December 2022 - the series' anniversary date - together with the switch to free-to-play. Steam's own app page confirms the model today: free, with in-app purchases, 700 MB of storage and a broadband line listed among the minimum requirements, because this is a game that lives on its servers.

On the shelf it sits as the complete line's live branch: the five classic wars stay single-purchase, archive-documented, each with its own rebuild named on its page - and Universe is where the series actually continues. Phone builds of it exist, but phone platforms stay outside this archive's scope, so the page documents the windows client only.
