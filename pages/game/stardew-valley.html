ABOUT STARDEW VALLEY

Stardew valley is the farming life sim built by one person: inherit the grandfather's plot, clear it, plant it, and let the valley's seasons structure the years - crops and livestock for income, the mines for combat, and a town whose friendship arcs, festivals and marriage candidates fill every week. The community center bundles and joja's alternative route give the progression its fork.

Concernedape shipped it in february 2016 and still maintains it; the steam listing is the current official door at full price with the 1.6 update line. The windows build is the original platform.

A light 2d pixel game - the haswell igpu bar is far above its needs. Not-tested stands until a run confirms install, launch and saves.
