ABOUT THE QUEEN OF FIGHTERS (NAKED MUGEN EDITION)

The queen of fighters (naked mugen edition) is tetchi's adult mugen project: an all-female roster assembled from sprite rips across fighting-game history, chained into a tag-battle mugen build with explicit defeat mechanics. It is the most famous of the mugen adult crossovers - a fan creation that documents the engine's whole culture as much as its own content.

The windows build was never sold; the archive route is the only door.
