ABOUT PARKING DASH

Parking Dash, released on 1 october 2008, stars Karma - Flo's friend from DinerTown - running a valet service, with development credited to kef sensei under playfirst. Cars queue at the stand, each driver with a patience meter, and every parked vehicle becomes a spatial puzzle to retrieve on demand.

The trade changes the verb set: driving lines replace walking routes, and the lot's layout matters as much as the queue order. It later reached steam in the playfirst batch of 2010 before that listing was pulled, which leaves the game without an official channel today.

The 2008 build is un-buyable - preserved through archives and hosted originals only.
