ABOUT CHICKEN INVADERS EPISODE 1: THE SAGA BEGINS

Chicken Invaders Episode 1: The Saga Begins is a brand-new version of the first war built on the studio's new engine, released inside the Steam package Chicken Invaders 1 on 7 March 2025. It is the opening chapter of the episodic rework - the same program that rebuilt wars two to five - and it carries many changes backported from Chicken Invaders Universe: new weapons, new special weapons, boosters, six skill levels where the original had three, custom cuisines and hot, frozen, electric, massive and darkness environments.

The package it rides is deliberately shaped: one purchase puts the 1999 original, the 2023 classic-engine remaster and this new-engine remake on the same shelf. The episodic format for the later wars works differently - those are free downloads for registered owners through the studio's site - but the first war's episode reached Steam first and ships as part of the bundle.

With this page the first war is complete on the shelf: the 1999 classic (archive truth), Remastered (2023, classic engine) and Episode 1 (new engine) - three builds of one war, each with its own address.
