ABOUT OKAMI

Okami casts you as amaterasu the sun goddess in wolf form, restoring a celestial-blighted nippon with the celestial brush - pause the world and paint suns, cherry bombs, vines and slashes into it. The sumi-e art direction is the game's identity, the dungeon-and-sidequest structure is classical zelda grammar, and the brush gimmick never runs out of applications.

Clover's original shipped on ps2 in 2006; capcom's okami hd port reached steam in december 2017 and is the current official door for pc, still sold. The page documents the hd door honestly: the 2006 original never shipped on windows, so this port is the platform's only route.

The hd port is a light 3d renderer - the haswell igpu bar holds it at 1080p60 with headroom. Not-tested stands until a run confirms install, launch and saves.
