ABOUT PORTAL

Portal is the first-person puzzle game compressed to its purest form: the aperture science handheld portal device, nineteen test chambers of momentum-and-paradox design, and GLaDOS's passive-aggressive narration running the whole thing. The orange-blue portal loop - fling, momentum, escape - is the cleanest mechanic statement valve ever shipped.

Valve shipped it in october 2007 inside the orange box, and the steam listing is the standalone door at a token price. It is the studio's own platform - the most official route pc gaming has.

Source 2007 is trivially under the haswell igpu bar at native settings. Not-tested stands until a run confirms install, launch and saves.
