ABOUT DOWNHILL DOMINATION

Downhill domination (2003) is the ps2's answer to the extreme-sports era: downhill mountain-bike races down real-scale mountains, combat allowed - kicks, punches, thrown water bottles - and speed earned by picking braver lines. The vertical drop design and the trick system hold up as the genre's best bike entry.
