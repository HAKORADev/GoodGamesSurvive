ABOUT COOKING DASH 3: THRILLS & SPILLS - COLLECTOR'S EDITION

The collector's edition of Cooking Dash 3: Thrills & Spills is the premium cut of the 2010 prequel that sent young flo back to a theme-park diner. Where the standard build ends its stage list, the collector's edition adds the Klondike Gold Rush - a full exclusive stage that only this package carries.

The bundle rounds out with a strategy guide, a screensaver and concept art, the same collector's-line formula playfirst used across its late-era catalog. Full walkthroughs of the exclusive stage exist - expert-mode runs of levels 59 and 60 are the klondike content in motion - which makes the edition's added value easy to see rather than take on faith.

As with every playfirst.com exclusive, the buy route vanished when the portal did, and steam never carried the collector's cut. The honest slot stays empty here; the standard Thrills & Spills build keeps the line's archive routes on its own page.
