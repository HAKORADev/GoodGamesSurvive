ABOUT DANTE'S INFERNO

Dante's inferno (2010) reworks the first canticle of the divine comedy as a heavy character-action game: dante carves through the nine circles of hell, each with its own hazards, bosses and judgment choices between holy and unholy upgrades. The psp port shipped the complete descent - all nine circles, all bosses - scaled to the handheld, and it stands as one of the last big-scale action ports the psp received.

No windows build exists, so the platform is psp - played through ppsspp, rom sourced from romsfun.
