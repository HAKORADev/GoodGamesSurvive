ABOUT KINGDOM HEARTS BIRTH BY SLEEP

Kingdom hearts birth by sleep (2010) is the prequel that rebuilt the series' whole timeline: terra, ventus and aqua chase the vanished master xehanort across disney worlds a decade before sora's story, and each of the three campaigns reframes the same events until the last chapter locks the tragedy in place. The command-deck combat system remains one of the series' best action kits, and mirage arena adds local multiplayer boss hunts.
