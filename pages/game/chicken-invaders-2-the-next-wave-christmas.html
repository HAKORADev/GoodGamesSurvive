ABOUT CHICKEN INVADERS 2: THE NEXT WAVE - CHRISTMAS EDITION

The Next Wave Christmas Edition (25 November 2003) is the game that started the Chicken Invaders edition tradition: take a full episode, repaint the invasion in snow and fairy lights, swap the food for holiday roast, and ship it as both a standalone release and a bundled extra for the mainline game.

Underneath the decoration it is the same fixed-shooter campaign as The Next Wave - same weapon tiers, same co-op, same escalating wave design - with the holiday dressing on top. That formula turned out to be the series' signature: InterAction has kept releasing these seasonal variants for two decades, and collectors treat the editions as games of their own.

This page covers the original 2003 edition; the Steam listing (2016) carries the remastered build of it. Either way it is the Christmas invasion, wave one through the end, with the fate of the holidays on one very cold spaceship.
