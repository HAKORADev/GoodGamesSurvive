ABOUT HALF-LIFE 2

Half-Life 2 is the physics-shooter that taught a generation what in-engine physics meant: the gravity gun turns props into weapons and puzzles, city 17's occupied streets carry the Combine's occupation with barely a cutscene, and the vehicle chapters stretch the same engine grammar across coast and highway. Its storytelling-through-design remains the series' core law.

Valve shipped it in november 2004, and the steam listing is the current official door at a token price - the original build with the episodes attached in the package structure. It is the studio's own platform, so the route is as official as pc gaming gets.
