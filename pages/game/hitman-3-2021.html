ABOUT HITMAN III

HITMAN 3 (20 January 2021) closes the World of Assassination trilogy at the top of its form: Dubai's Burj Al-Ghazali opening with the two Providence partners Carl Ingram and Marcus Stuyvesant, Dartmoor's murder-mystery mansion, Berlin's rave in the ruins, the Carpathian train finale. The story settles 47's origin, frees Diana into open war against Providence, and ends the arc that Codename 47 started in 2000.

As a sandbox it is the trilogy's sharpest - workers-only zones, the return of shortcuts, camera-based chains, and Freelancer (2023), a roguelike campaign mode added post-launch that reuses the trilogy's maps in randomized runs. Tamara Vidal and Arthur Edwards carry the final contracts; Lucas Grey's arc resolves mid-season and the fallout drives the last act.

The ownership story is the modern case study: on 26 January 2023 IOI rebranded HITMAN 3 as HITMAN World of Assassination, delisted the 2016 and 2018 standalone releases, and made this one store page the entire trilogy's legal home. That page is this game's official link now. Sixty gigabytes and a GTX 660 floor keep it off the target machine - the archive watches the classics; this is where the series lives today.
