ABOUT THIS COLLECTION

One hundred percent of the classic Chicken Invaders line: the five mainline episodes and every holiday edition the series ever shipped - Christmas x4, Easter x2, Halloween x1, Thanksgiving x1 - thirteen builds in release order, from the 1999 original to the classic line's 2016 Christmas bow.

The production quirk the shelf keeps honest: the third war's Christmas Edition (November 2006) shipped before the standard game (January 2007), making it the only edition in the series that predates its own episode.

InterAction studios is alive and still distributes most of this line itself, which makes these pages ownership stories rather than rescues - the exception is the Ultimate Omelette holiday trio, never carried on Steam and living only on the studio's own download hub.
