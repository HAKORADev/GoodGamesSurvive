The Chicken Invaders Collection

the classic line: five wars, 1999 to 2014, every holiday release riding as a version.

5 pages · 2 catalogued-only

ABOUT THIS COLLECTION

One hundred percent of the classic Chicken Invaders line the owner listed: the five mainline episodes, with every holiday release the series shipped living as a version under its episode's page - Christmas x4, Easter x2, Halloween x1, Thanksgiving x1 - switched from the dropdown, not scattered as standalone catalog entries.

The production quirk the shelf keeps honest: the third war's Christmas Edition (November 2006) shipped before the standard game (January 2007), making it the only edition in the series that predates its own episode.

InterAction studios is alive and every entry on this shelf is still obtainable through an official channel: the studio's own download hub carries the whole line, and Steam sells the mainline episodes and all eight holiday editions (every app page and price verified 2026-10-09). these pages are ownership stories, not rescues - the archive links ride along for preservation.
