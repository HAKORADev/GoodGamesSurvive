ABOUT THIS COLLECTION

Every numbered Hitman, in order: Codename 47's origin, Silent Assassin's conscience, Contracts' dark detour, Blood Money's sandbox peak, Absolution's cinematic detour, and the World of Assassination trilogy that rebuilt everything and merged into one release in 2023.

The classics (2000-2006) are true archive targets - they run on and under the haswell iGPU bar and are still sold DRM-free. Absolution sits at the target line with its 24GB body. The modern trilogy is catalogued as the series' upgrade path: it will not fit the target machine, and its pages exist to route ownership, not to pretend otherwise.
