ABOUT DUCKSTATION

Duckstation is the playstation emulator this site's ps1 vault runs on: upscaled 3d, widescreen patches, save states and the most accurate software renderer in the ps1 field. The official site serves the current builds for windows without pinning a version.

One law: a ps1 bios image is required for real compatibility, and duckstation ships none - dump it from your own console and load it once in settings; the project's wiki carries the details. Everything else about setup is two minutes of work.
