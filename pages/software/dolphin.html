ABOUT DOLPHIN

Dolphin is the gamecube and wii emulator this site's wii vault runs on: the official download page serves the current release and development builds, and the emulator carries the console's library from super mario galaxy 2 to the zanpakuto fighters with native resolution scaling and texture packs on top.

It needs no bios dumps - the gamecube and wii firmware handling is emulated in-house, which keeps setup to two clicks. The link stays semi-direct to the official download page; the wiki carries the per-game compatibility answers.
