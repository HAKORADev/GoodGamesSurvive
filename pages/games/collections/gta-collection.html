ABOUT THIS COLLECTION

The grand theft auto row this catalogue carries runs three pages: the classic san andreas & vice city pairing, gta iv and gta v. The direct line reads in order - vice city (2002), san andreas (2004) as the 3d-era peaks bundled together here, then gta iv (2008) rebooting the tone in liberty city, then gta v (2013/2015) splitting the lead three ways in los santos.

Each step is a sequel, not an upgrade: none of these replaces another, and the classics stay the only versions of themselves since rockstar delisted them. The bundle page documents both classic builds; the remaster-era definitive editions live outside this catalogue and are named where relevant.
