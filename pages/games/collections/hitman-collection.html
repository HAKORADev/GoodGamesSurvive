ABOUT THIS COLLECTION

The Hitman games the archive's list actually names: Silent Assassin's conscience, Contracts' dark detour, Blood Money's sandbox peak, Absolution's cinematic detour. The full lineage - Codename 47 (2000) through the World of Assassination line (2016-2023) - lives on every page's series strip, so the complete family is always one glance away even where its pages are not dug yet.

The classics (2002-2006) run on and under the haswell iGPU bar and are still sold DRM-free. Absolution sits at the target line with its 24GB body. Every page carries the same law: official path first where one exists, the archive route right next to it.

The remaster truth: the series' only classic-line remaster is the Hitman HD Enhanced Collection (2019) - Blood Money + Absolution rebuilt at 4K/60 for PlayStation 4 and Xbox One, never windows. Silent Assassin and Contracts were never remastered at all. The windows builds on this shelf are therefore not stepping stones - they are the final windows truth.
