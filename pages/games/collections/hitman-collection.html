ABOUT THIS COLLECTION

Every Hitman entry the catalogue carries, in one place: Silent Assassin (2002), Contracts (2004), Blood Money (2006) and Absolution (2012) - the four windows builds this archive digs - plus the two console remaster lines that sit above them. The 2013 Hitman HD Trilogy rebuilt Silent Assassin, Contracts and Blood Money for ps3 and xbox 360; the 2019 Hitman HD Enhanced Collection rebuilt Blood Money and Absolution at 4k/60 for ps4 and xbox one. Neither remaster ever shipped a windows build, so on pc the four originals remain the real thing - both remaster pages document their lines honestly from the console side.

Codename 47, the World of Assassination trilogy and the spin-offs (GO, Sniper, Sniper Challenge) sit outside the current catalogue; they appear in the series strips as reference only. Six pages, one contract.
