ABOUT THIS COLLECTION

Four of the mainline Hitman games, covering the series' 2002-2012 decade: Silent Assassin, Contracts, Blood Money and Absolution. All four are still sold - the classics (2002-2006) DRM-free on Steam and GOG, Absolution on Steam. The wider lineage - Codename 47 (2000) and the World of Assassination line (2016-2023) - rides the series strip of every page as reference.

The classics run on and under the haswell iGPU bar and remain sold DRM-free. Absolution anchors the shelf with its 24 GB footprint. Where an official channel exists it comes first on the page; the archive routes sit next to it.

Two console remasters complete the picture, and neither shipped for windows: Hitman HD Trilogy (2013) remastered Silent Assassin, Contracts and Blood Money for PS3 and Xbox 360, and Hitman HD Enhanced Collection (2019) rebuilt Blood Money and Absolution at 4K/60 for PS4 and Xbox One. The windows builds on this shelf are therefore the definitive way to play these four on PC.
