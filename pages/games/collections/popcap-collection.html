ABOUT THIS COLLECTION

PopCap's deluxe era is the spine of the casual shelf: zuma, peggle, bejeweled 3, chuzzle, dynomite, insaniquarium, heavy weapon, plants vs. zombies, plus the post-merger doors for astropop, big money and talismania, and the archive-only stock the current stores never carried - bookworm adventures, atomica, pixelus, qbeez whirled, seven seas, tiptop, venice and rocket mania.

Every row keeps its own page; a handful also sit in the marble poppers collection because the mechanics put them there. The collection view is exactly that - one shelf, sorted by nothing but belonging.
