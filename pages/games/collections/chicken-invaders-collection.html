ABOUT THIS COLLECTION

The full Chicken Invaders line from InterAction studios: the five classic wars with every holiday release living as a version under its episode's page (four Christmas editions, two Easter editions, one Halloween, one Thanksgiving - switched from the dropdown, not scattered as standalone entries), plus the two things that complete the family: the remaster line and Universe.

One production quirk stays on record: the third war's Christmas Edition (November 2006) shipped three months before the standard game (January 2007), making it the only edition in the series that predates its own episode.

InterAction studios is alive and every entry remains officially obtainable: the studio's download hub carries the classic line, Steam sells the mainline episodes and all eight holiday editions, and Universe is free to play. These pages are ownership stories, not rescues - the archive links ride along for preservation.

A sequel is never an upgrade, and the pages keep that law: each classic episode's upgrade line names its own rebuild - the first two wars received classic-engine remasters (2012 and 2023), while the later episodes ride the Universe-engine reworks. The remaster page carries the full line, entry by entry.
