the complete line: five wars, their remasters written one by one, and the universe - 1999 to today.

ABOUT THIS COLLECTION

The full Chicken Invaders line from InterAction studios: the five classic wars with every holiday release living as a version under its episode's page (four Christmas editions, two Easter editions, one Halloween, one Thanksgiving - switched from the dropdown, not scattered as standalone entries), plus the three things that complete the family: the per-episode remasters, the episodic rework, and Universe.

One production quirk stays on record: the third war's Christmas Edition (November 2006) shipped three months before the standard game (January 2007), making it the only edition in the series that predates its own episode.

InterAction studios is alive and every entry remains officially obtainable: the studio's download hub carries the classic line, Steam sells the mainline episodes and five of the eight holiday editions as DLC (the second and third wars' Christmas editions, the fourth war's Thanksgiving, and both fifth-war editions - the rest live on the studio's own store), and Universe is free to play. These pages are ownership stories, not rescues - the archive links ride along for preservation.

A sequel is never an upgrade, and the pages keep that law one war at a time: the first war's rebuild is Chicken Invaders Remastered (2023), the second war's is The Next Wave Remastered (2012), and the later wars ride the Episodes rework (2025) instead - no classic-engine remaster was ever made for wars three to five. Every classic page names its own rebuild, and every rebuild has its own page.
