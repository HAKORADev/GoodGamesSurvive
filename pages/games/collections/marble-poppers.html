the chain-shooter line - zuma's law, luxor's temples and every track that rolls.

13 pages

ABOUT THIS COLLECTION

Marble shooters run one law: a chain rolls, you shoot, three of a color bursts. This collection carries every windows build of that law in the catalogue - zuma deluxe and zuma's revenge, the full luxor line from the 2004 original to 5th passage, 10tons' sparkle and sparkle unleashed, and the portal-era variants beetle bug 3, karu and charma.

Zuma's two entries also sit in the popcap collection - a game can belong to more than one shelf, and the pages say so in their collections line.
