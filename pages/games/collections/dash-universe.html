The Dash Universe

PlayFirst's DinerTown empire: every Dash game of the pc era, one universe.

31 pages

ABOUT THIS COLLECTION

Diner Dash (2003) by gameLab and PlayFirst turned restaurant time-management into a franchise and its waitress Flo into a casual-gaming icon. The pc line it built runs a full decade: Restaurant Rescue (2006), Flo on the Go (2006), Hometown Hero (2007) - with the Once Upon a Diner story expansion and the standalone Seasonal Snack Pack riding beside it - Flo Through Time (2008) and Boom! (2010) carry the numbered story, and the formula spun off into whole sub-lines - Wedding Dash ran 2007-2010 across four games, Cooking Dash 2008-2010 across three (its Thrills & Spills entry shipped a collector's edition with an exclusive Klondike Gold Rush stage), Hotel Dash 2009-2011 across two, and Avenue Flo (2009-2010) turned the waitress into a point-and-click adventurer. The collector's-edition line matters here: Boom!'s 2012 collector's edition added five exclusive advanced levels and a strategy guide, and it has its own page through the Boom! entry.

The DinerTown side line filled out the town itself: Doggie, Dairy, Fashion, Parking, Fitness, Diaper and Garden Dash (2008-2011) moved the chained-service engine to spas, farms, studios, lots, gyms, daycares and gardens, DinerTown Tycoon (2009) stretched it into a business sim, DinerTown Detective Agency (2009) went hidden-object, Soap Opera Dash (2008) ran the loop on a tv studio set, and two licensed SpongeBob SquarePants crossovers (2006-2007) carried it onto Nickelodeon's turf. Thirty-one titles, one universe, all of it the pc era.

The shelf's state is mixed and honest about it: seven of the thirty-one still sell on Steam (Hometown Hero, Cooking Dash, Wedding Dash 2, Hotel Dash: Suite Success, Fitness Dash, Diaper Dash and DinerTown Tycoon), while the rest died with their channels - PlayFirst folded into Glu Mobile in 2014, playfirst.com is gone, and the download portals closed with it. The un-buyable cases are the archive's rescue lane: no store, no key server, preserved anyway.

Scope law for this collection: windows builds only. The mobile free-to-play era that followed the classic line is outside this collection - the Deluxe re-releases (Diner Dash Deluxe, SpongeBob Diner Dash Deluxe) and Diner Dash Rush, Diner Dash (2014) and Diner Dash Adventures all belong to phones, and phones are out of scope for this archive. Each entry below is its own dug page with its own sources, sizes and archive routes.
