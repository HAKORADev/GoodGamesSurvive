The Dash Universe

PlayFirst's DinerTown empire: every Dash game from the 2003 original to the Glu era, one universe.

1 pages · 28 non-ready pages

ABOUT THIS COLLECTION

Diner Dash (2003) by gameLab and PlayFirst turned restaurant time-management into a franchise and its waitress Flo into a casual-gaming icon. The main line held for a decade: Restaurant Rescue (2006), Flo on the Go (2006), Hometown Hero (2007), Flo Through Time (2008) and Boom! (2010).

The formula spun off fast. Wedding Dash ran 2007-2010 across four games, Cooking Dash 2008-2010 across three, and Hotel Dash 2009-2011 across two, each moving Flo's diner formula to a new trade. The DinerTown side line - Doggie, Dairy, Fashion, Parking, Fitness, Diaper and Garden Dash (2008-2011), plus Avenue Flo (2009-2010) and DinerTown Tycoon (2009) - filled out the town itself, and two licensed SpongeBob Diner Dash games (2006-2007) carried the formula onto Nickelodeon's turf.

The platform shift ended the classic era. PlayFirst folded into Glu Mobile in 2014, the original PC builds left circulation, and the series went free-to-play on mobile with Diner Dash (2014) and Diner DASH Adventures (2019). The classic windows builds were never re-released digitally, and the era's unlock servers are long gone - which makes this shelf an archive story first.

One page is dug so far: the 2003 original, with its rescue story and its unlock-server law. The rest of the universe is catalogued below, each entry with its own row, year and platform.
